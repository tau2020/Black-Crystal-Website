Infrastructure and construction drive growth, and they shape the daily lives of the communities who use and live alongside new facilities. Our 2024 profile records Environmental and Social Site Assessments, Environmental Prospectuses and Environmental Management Plans for health centres for NMSI Infrastructure Limited and the Ministry of Health and Child Care (2021–2024), effluent treatment plants and a training centre for Padenga, a proposed explosives manufacturing plant, a builders' warehouse, an office complex, a fuel service station and mixed land-use developments in and around Harare.

## How we support infrastructure clients

- Environmental Prospectuses, ESIAs and site assessments for new facilities
- Environmental Management Plans for construction and operation
- Scoping reports for feasibility studies, including large-scale irrigation
- Addenda when project designs change
- Technical review of EIAs for public infrastructure
