Tourism in southern Africa depends on healthy ecosystems, wildlife and cultural landscapes. Our 2024 profile records tourism and conservation work from Victoria Falls and Hwange National Park to the Zambezi Valley, Umfurudzi and the Save Valley Conservancy.

Assignments include Environmental Impact Assessments and Environmental Prospectuses for new and upgraded tourism facilities; an EIA for an airstrip supporting tourist access and anti-poaching patrols; an EIA and wildlife management programme for the re-introduction of wild animals into Umfurudzi Safari Area; technical environmental and social advice to the Save Valley Conservancy; and a report on the biodiversity, conservation and cultural heritage importance of the Mavhuradonha Wilderness Area, which was gazetted as a National Monument in early 2017.

## How we support tourism and conservation clients

- EIAs, Environmental Prospectuses and management plans for lodges, camps and facilities
- Biodiversity and wildlife management input
- Cultural heritage assessment
- Technical EIA reviews for projects in sensitive areas
- Technical environmental and social advisory for conservancies
