Minerals and mining is one of Black Crystal's specialist sectors, and mining assignments make up a substantial part of the project history recorded in our 2024 company profile — from exploration and prospecting through feasibility, expansion and operational support.

Documented mining work includes Environmental and Social Impact Assessments for platinum mining for Bravura Zimbabwe (2023–2024), Global Platinum Resources (2022–2023) and Karo Platinum (2021–2022, to IFC standards); a series of assessments for Anglo American's Unki Mine in Shurugwi; socio-economic reviews using the Socio-Economic Assessment Toolbox (SEAT); legal register reviews; and technical reviews of EIAs for diamond, coal, nickel and quarry projects.

## How we support mining clients

- ESIAs, EIAs and Environmental Prospectuses for prospecting, mining and supporting infrastructure
- Social risk, displacement and relocation-related studies
- Legal compliance reviews, gap analyses and permitting support
- Mine closure planning and rehabilitation input
- Environmental and stakeholder-management training for site teams
- Independent technical review of EIA reports
