Conventional and renewable energy is one of our specialist sectors. Our 2024 profile records assessments for solar facilities ranging from 150 kW to 300 MW, a wind project in Guruve, a Renewable Energy Technology Park in Chipinge, hydroelectric schemes including the proposed Batoka Gorge Hydro-Electric Scheme on the Zambezi River, and transmission infrastructure such as the 2nd Alaska–Sherwood 400/330 kV line.

We have also worked on existing conventional generation, including an Environmental and Social Audit and Management Plan for the upgrading of Hwange Power Station and a Biodiversity Management Plan for the station.

## How we support energy clients

- ESIAs, Environmental Prospectuses and Environmental Management Plans for generation and transmission projects
- Assessments structured to address IFC standards where projects seek international finance
- Relocation Action Plans and displacement studies for linear infrastructure and reservoirs
- Biodiversity management plans and ecological input
- Environmental and social audits of operating facilities
- IFC gap reviews and scopes of work for existing assessments
