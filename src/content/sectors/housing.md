Housing is listed among Black Crystal's specialist sectors in our 2024 company profile. Residential and mixed-use developments change how land is used for generations, so environmental and social considerations need to shape layouts, services and construction from the start.

Related documented work includes an Environmental Prospectus for a mixed land-use development comprising a hotel, retail and private apartments on the outskirts of Harare's Central Business District (2013). Other mixed land-use work is listed on our [Infrastructure & Construction](/sectors/infrastructure/) page.

## How we support housing developers

- Environmental Prospectuses, ESIAs and Environmental Management Plans
- Site suitability, soils and land-use input
- Social and community health and safety considerations
- GIS mapping of constraints and sensitive features
- Construction-phase environmental management
