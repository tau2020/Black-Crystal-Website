Southern Africa's landscapes hold thousands of years of human history, from archaeological sites to graves, shrines and places that remain central to community identity today. Development that overlooks this heritage risks irreversible losses, community conflict and costly delays.

Black Crystal's 2024 company profile describes how the company advises clients on managing environmental, archaeological and social risks during the implementation of development projects, and lists archaeological and cultural heritage assessments among its core services.

## A case for early identification

Heritage resources are easiest to protect when they are known before layouts are fixed. A baseline survey at feasibility stage can steer roads, pits, pylons and buildings away from sensitive places; a discovery during construction is far more disruptive.

## Heritage is also living

Assessments should not stop at archaeology. Graves, sacred sites and landscapes with spiritual meaning are often known only to local communities and custodians. Respectful consultation is the only reliable way to find them, and it builds the trust that projects need.

## Landscapes of shared value

Our profile records a report Black Crystal compiled on the _Biodiversity, Conservation and Cultural Heritage Importance of the Mavhuradonha Wilderness Area_ in Muzarabani district — an area of high biodiversity, conservation and cultural heritage value that was under threat from uncontrolled environmental degradation caused by prospecting and mining. In early 2017 the area was gazetted as a National Monument by the National Museums and Monuments of Zimbabwe.

The case illustrates how ecological and cultural values often overlap, and why assessing them together gives decision-makers a fuller picture.

## Good practice in brief

- Include heritage specialists at scoping, not only after layouts are fixed
- Combine desktop research, field survey and community consultation
- Agree chance-find procedures with contractors before ground is broken
- Engage heritage authorities early
