Lasting environmental change depends on people understanding why it matters. Our training and education services build environmental awareness and practical management skills, from schools and communities to company teams.

Documented training in our 2024 profile includes environmental awareness training for contract environmental officers on the Unki Platinum project site (2008), a management training workshop series on stakeholder management for Unki Platinum Mines (2014), and support for a training workshop on BROA, an internationally accredited Earthwatch Institute programme hosted by Rift Valley Corporation (2015).

## Typical programmes

- Environmental awareness sessions for communities, women and youth
- Conservation education for schools and children
- Company environmental management training for managers, supervisors and environmental officers
- Stakeholder-management workshops for project teams
- Awareness materials and newsletters
