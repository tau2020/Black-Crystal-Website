Biodiversity assessments and ecological surveys establish which species, habitats and ecosystem services are present on and around a site, how sensitive they are, and how a project could affect them over time.

Documented work in our 2024 profile includes a Biodiversity Management Plan for Hwange Power Station (2015, with Sustainability Australia), a report on the biodiversity, conservation and cultural heritage importance of the Mavhuradonha Wilderness Area (2017), a wildlife management programme with environmental monitoring for the re-introduction of wild animals into Umfurudzi Safari Area (2011), and vegetation biomass fieldwork for a REDD+ forest carbon project in northern Zimbabwe (from 2010).

## Typical scope

- Desktop review of existing records and protected-area information
- Field surveys of vegetation, habitats and fauna, planned for the season and site
- Identification of sensitive habitats and species of conservation concern
- Impact assessment and mitigation following the mitigation hierarchy
- Biodiversity management and monitoring plans
- Ecological monitoring during construction and operation
