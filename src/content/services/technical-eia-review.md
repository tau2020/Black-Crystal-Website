Regulators, conservancies and project owners often need an independent view on whether an Environmental Impact Assessment report is adequate. A technical EIA review tests the scope, methods, findings and proposed mitigation of a report, and sets out what is missing or needs to be strengthened.

Our 2024 profile records technical reviews carried out between 2010 and 2023, including reviews on behalf of the Environmental Management Agency (EMA) for diamond mining, an Engen project and the Harare airport road, and reviews for Malilangwe Trust of proposed coal mining and coal bed methane exploration projects.

## Typical scope

- Review of the EIA against applicable legal requirements and terms of reference
- Evaluation of baseline data, impact assessment methods and significance ratings
- Assessment of mitigation, management and monitoring commitments
- Identification of gaps and recommendations for additional work
- A clear, structured review report for decision-makers
