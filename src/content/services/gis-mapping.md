Good environmental decisions depend on understanding where things are. Geographical Information Systems (GIS) mapping brings spatial data together (land use, vegetation, water, infrastructure, communities and sensitive features) into clear maps and analyses that support assessment, planning and monitoring.

Geographical Information Systems mapping is listed among our Specialised Services in the 2024 company profile.

## Typical outputs

- Study-area and site-location maps
- Land use, vegetation and habitat mapping
- Sensitivity and constraints mapping to guide project layout
- Mapping of affected households, assets and infrastructure for social studies
- Maps and figures for ESIA reports, management plans and monitoring
