Environmental and social audits test how an operating facility or project is performing against its legal obligations, permit conditions, management plans and company policies, and identify what needs to change.

The work recorded in our 2024 profile includes an Environmental and Social Audit and Environmental and Social Management Plan for the upgrading of Hwange Power Station (2013, in partnership with Epoch Resources and Sustainability Australia) and an environmental gap analysis and permits acquisition for platinum mining at Todal Mining's Bokai Mine (2024).

## Typical scope

- Review of permits, licences, management plans and previous audit findings
- Site inspections and interviews with operational staff and, where relevant, community representatives
- Assessment of performance against legal requirements and lender or corporate standards
- Gap analysis with prioritised corrective actions
- Updated management plans and monitoring arrangements

## When audits are used

Audits are commonly required for operating facilities, requested by lenders and investors during due diligence, or commissioned internally before expansions, acquisitions or the renewal of environmental licences.
