Soils and land use shape what a site can sustainably support. Soils and land use assessments characterise soil types, land capability and current land uses, so that projects can minimise erosion, protect productive land and plan rehabilitation.

Soils and land use assessment is listed among our Assessment & Analysis services in the 2024 profile. Related documented fieldwork includes soil carbon sampling and the preliminary analysis of soil data for a REDD+ forest carbon project in northern Zimbabwe (from 2010).

## Typical scope

- Soil survey and sampling, with classification and mapping
- Land capability and current land-use assessment
- Erosion risk and topsoil management planning
- Input to rehabilitation, closure and agricultural plans
- Mapping of land-use change
