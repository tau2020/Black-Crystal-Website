Large projects bring new traffic, workers, dust, noise, water demand and disease risks into nearby communities. Community health and safety assessments look at these effects from the community's perspective and set out how they should be prevented and managed.

Community health and safety assessments are listed among our Community Impact services in the 2024 company profile.

## Typical scope

- Baseline review of community health, infrastructure and vulnerable groups
- Assessment of risks such as traffic and transport, hazardous materials, water and sanitation, disease transmission, worker influx and emergency scenarios
- Engagement with communities, local authorities and health services
- Community health and safety management and emergency response measures
- Monitoring indicators and reporting
