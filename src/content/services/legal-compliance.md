Environmental legal requirements change, overlap and differ by sector. Legal compliance auditing and environmental legal reviews give organisations a clear picture of which obligations apply to them and how well they are meeting them.

Our 2024 profile lists legal compliance auditing (under Assessment & Analysis) and environmental legal reviews (under Specialised Services). Documented work includes an Occupational Health and Safety and Environmental Legal Registers review for a platinum mine in Shurugwi (2013) and an environmental gap analysis and permits acquisition for Todal Mining's Bokai Mine (2024).

## Typical scope

- Identification of applicable environmental legislation, regulations, permits and licence conditions
- Preparation and review of legal registers
- Compliance audits against permits and statutory requirements
- Gap analysis and support with permit applications and renewals
- Practical recommendations for closing compliance gaps

> We provide technical environmental compliance advice. It is not a substitute for a formal legal opinion from a qualified legal practitioner.
