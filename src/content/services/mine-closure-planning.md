Responsible mining plans for closure from the start. Mine closure planning sets out how a site will be made safe, stable and non-polluting after operations end, how land will be rehabilitated, and how the social transition will be managed for workers and communities.

Mine closure planning is listed among our Specialised Services in the 2024 company profile. The mining work recorded in the profile covers platinum, gold, chrome and quarry operations, together with technical reviews of diamond, coal and nickel projects.

## Typical scope

- Review of the mine plan, permits and existing closure commitments
- Closure objectives and post-closure land-use options developed with stakeholders
- Rehabilitation and revegetation planning, including topsoil management
- Identification of residual risks such as slope stability, water quality and contamination
- Progressive rehabilitation schedules and monitoring
- Closure cost considerations and periodic plan updates
