Projects succeed or fail on their relationships with the people they affect. Social risk assessments examine how a development could change livelihoods, land use, access to resources and community health and wellbeing, and how those risks can be managed fairly.

The 2024 profile records social and resettlement-related assignments including Relocation Action Plans for the proposed Batoka Gorge Hydro-Electric Scheme (2018, in partnership with Environmental Resources Management) and the 2nd Alaska–Sherwood 400/330 kV transmission line (2017–2018), a Displacement Impact Verification Study for a mine solar project (2023–2024), and reviews using the Socio-Economic Assessment Toolbox (SEAT) for a platinum mine in Shurugwi.

## Typical scope

- Socio-economic baseline studies and stakeholder mapping
- Stakeholder engagement and consultation planning
- Assessment of displacement, livelihood and access impacts
- Relocation and resettlement planning support
- Social management plans, grievance procedures and monitoring indicators
- Reviews of existing social performance tools and plans
