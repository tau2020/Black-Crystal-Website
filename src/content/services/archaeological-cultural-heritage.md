Development can disturb archaeological sites, graves, sacred places and landscapes that carry deep cultural meaning. Archaeological and cultural heritage assessments identify these resources early so that they can be avoided, protected or appropriately managed.

Archaeological and cultural heritage assessments are listed among our core Assessment & Analysis services. Our 2024 profile also records our report on the _Biodiversity, Conservation and Cultural Heritage Importance of the Mavhuradonha Wilderness Area_ in Muzarabani district; the area was gazetted as a National Monument by the National Museums and Monuments of Zimbabwe in early 2017.

## Typical scope

- Desktop and archival research
- Field surveys to identify archaeological and heritage resources
- Consultation with communities and custodians on living heritage, including graves and sacred sites
- Assessment of significance and of potential impacts
- Heritage management measures, including chance-find procedures for construction
- Liaison with heritage authorities as required

Heritage requirements are confirmed for each project with the relevant authorities; in Zimbabwe these include the National Museums and Monuments of Zimbabwe.
