Environmental responsibility extends beyond assessments. Our Community Impact services include participating in clean-up campaigns and working with partners on the up-scaling and recycling of waste such as glass, paper and clothing.

Since 2019 Black Crystal has run a series of national clean-up campaigns in line with the Presidential clean-up initiative (Company Profile 2024). We believe that together we can make the planet cleaner: reduce, reuse, recycle.

## How we contribute

- Organising and taking part in community and workplace clean-up campaigns
- Connecting organisations with partners who up-scale or recycle waste streams such as glass, paper and clothing
- Combining practical action with environmental awareness
- Supporting corporate social responsibility and staff engagement initiatives
