Sustainability advisory helps organisations understand their environmental and social performance, report on it credibly and turn commitments into practical projects.

The 2024 profile records sustainability reporting for Dallaglio Investments (2021) and INSCOR Group (2021–2022); an evaluation of a company's sustainability report on its journey to integrated reporting, in association with Paramark (2017–2018), using the Integrated Reporting Framework, the Global Reporting Initiative and the ISO range of standards; sustainable living project concepts for Unilever Zimbabwe (2012); a greenhouse gas study and climate change adaptation for a platinum mine (2011); and technical advisory work on a REDD+ forest carbon project in northern Zimbabwe (from 2010).

## Typical scope

- Sustainability and integrated reporting support
- Gap reviews of existing reports against recognised frameworks
- Identification of material issues and key performance indicators
- Greenhouse gas and climate change adaptation studies
- Sustainability and sustainable-living project concepts
- Technical advisory support for conservation and carbon projects
