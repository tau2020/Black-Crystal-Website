# Asset provenance

Every image, video and brand file shipped by the site, where it came from, what was done to it, where it is used and what still needs confirming. The image registry in `src/data/media.ts` is the code-side counterpart: each entry records alt text and an `origin` of `company-profile-2024` or `ai-illustrative`.

**Rights status legend**

- **Approved:** supplied and approved by the owner for this use.
- **Confirm:** supplied by the client (extracted from the 2024 company profile), but web reuse rights are not yet confirmed. Presence in a PDF is not a web licence.
- **Illustrative (AI):** AI-generated concept imagery from the asset pack, approved for concept use only. It must never be presented as Black Crystal staff, clients, sites or outcomes.

No stock photography was added. No Mahlatini or other template imagery is used.

### Notes

- **Removed from the asset pack selection:**
  - `hero-river-valley*.webp` (replaced by the elephant video; the main version also has decorative contours baked in).
  - `02-sectors/infrastructure.webp` (baked-in label, smeared upscale).
  - `mining-illustrative.webp` (very low resolution).
  - `footer-tree-sunset.webp` and the AI rock-art project image (unused, or risk of implying an authenticated site).
  - `social-share-og.jpg` (showed the superseded river-valley concept).
- **Identifiable content:** the haul-truck photograph (p. 7) shows contractor branding ("DT-0123", "FMC") and two workers. The community-event (p. 25) and tree-planting (p. 24) photographs show people. These are the profile's own images, but confirm consent and rights before launch.
- **Captions:** every project page states "Illustrative image. It does not depict this project or its site." The featured-projects section carries the same disclaimer.
- **Resolution:** all profile-derived crops are cut at native resolution; nothing is upscaled (the build also refuses to upscale). The AI concept-board images are soft by origin and should be replaced with commissioned photography for a sharp launch (priority: Housing card, fieldwork portrait, community and training cards).
- **Fonts:** Nunito Sans (SIL OFL 1.1), self-hosted; licence in `src/assets/fonts/OFL-NunitoSans.txt`. No Avenir, Peace Sans or template fonts are shipped.
- **Icons:** 12 from the asset pack (`07-ui`, project-created) and 33 drawn for this project in the same style. No third-party icon library is used.
